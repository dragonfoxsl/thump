The blind spot

Your monitor stops at the network boundary.

Public uptime checks work well for public endpoints. They cannot see a nightly backup that never ran, an API on 10.0.0.0/8, or a dependency deliberately kept off the internet.

Thump observes those signals where they live and translates them into the smallest contract an uptime vendor understands.

01 / heartbeat
$ backup-nightly
last signal   26h 14m ago
deadline missed

The job that never ran

A silent cron failure has no public endpoint to fail. The job checks in; Thump notices when the expected signal never arrives.

02 / probe
$ probe payments.internal
target   10.0.4.21:8080
reachable from here

The service that should stay private

Probe internal services from the network where they are reachable—without giving an external monitoring vendor access to the target.

How it works

Bridge the gap.
Don't replace your stack.

Thump does the seeing. Your uptime vendor keeps doing the paging.

  1. 01

    Observe

    Send a cron heartbeat or run a probe where the private target is reachable.

  2. 02

    Record

    Thump stores the latest result and the observation history for each check.

  3. 03

    Expose

    Each check gets a plain status URL: 200 healthy or 503 failing.

  4. 04

    Alert

    Your existing uptime vendor polls that URL and follows the notification rules you already trust.

FAQ

Small tool.
Clear boundaries.

Does Thump replace my uptime provider?

No. Thump bridges checks your provider cannot reach directly. Your existing provider continues handling polling, notifications, and escalation.

Does my provider need private-network access?

No. It polls the public status endpoint exposed by Thump rather than connecting to the private target.

What can Thump monitor today?

Cron and scheduled-job heartbeats, plus HTTP services that Thump can actively probe from inside the network.

Can I self-host it without limits?

Yes. The open-source edition has no artificial check limit. You operate the service, persistence, ingress, and upgrades.

Is Thump Cloud available?

Not yet. It is a proposed managed option being validated with design partners. The public roadmap does not imply a launch date or finalized price.
